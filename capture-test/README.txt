Universal Video Course Recorder (v0.7.0)
========================================

HOW TO INSTALL & TEST IN CHROME:
--------------------------------
1. In Google Chrome, go to: chrome://extensions
2. Make sure "Developer mode" (top right corner toggle) is ON.
3. Find "Universal Video Course Recorder" and click the RELOAD icon (circle arrow).
   (Or click "Load unpacked" and select the recorder-test-extension folder).
4. Open your course video page (e.g. app.iskills.com or learn.sarmaaya.pk).
5. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar:
   - Your Windows Taskbar and Chrome browser tabs remain completely visible!
   - Video will smoothly scroll into view and auto-play.
   - While playing, video expands cleanly into Theater Mode inside the tab for pristine 1080p capture.
   - If auto-play is paused, the HUD has a direct [▶ Play] button so you can start it with one click.
   - Live Advance Buffer indicator (e.g. ⚡ 3m 45s buf) shows cached video ahead.
6. When the lecture finishes (or when you click the Stop button on the HUD / extension icon):
   - The video is saved directly to your Downloads/FrameCaptureTests folder.
   - The page and player controls restore to their normal state.
